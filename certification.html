The process

Five steps from application to certificate

Application

Submit company, facility, ingredient and product details.

Evaluation

HCA evaluates the application and supporting documents.

Agreement & quote

Certification agreement is signed and a price quote issued.

On-site audit

Auditors inspect your facility against halal requirements.

Decision

The Halal Certification Committee grants or declines certification.

What the on-site audit covers

  • Opening meeting — scope, schedule and audit team introductions.
  • Document review — ingredient declarations, supplier certificates, SOPs.
  • Plant inspection — step-by-step walk-through of production lines, storage and sanitation.
  • Closing meeting — findings discussed with your team.
  • Audit report — formal report submitted to the certification committee.

When certification may be refused

HCA may refuse to grant certification, or decline to issue a compliance document, where:

  • The client does not comply, or fails to continue complying, with halal requirements, halal standards, or Islamic law.
  • The client fails to meet obligations under the halal certification agreement.

All decisions on granting, maintaining, renewing, extending, reducing, suspending or withdrawing certification are made by the Halal Certification Committee, and clients are notified immediately. Suspended or withdrawn certificates become invalid from the notification date.

After certification

Maintaining, extending and renewing your certificate

Maintain

Keep complying with halal requirements. HCA may conduct surveillance audits to confirm your facility continues to meet the standard.

Extend

Adding products or lines? Submit an extension request — evaluation and committee approval are required, sometimes with a surveillance audit.

Renew

Certificates are time-limited. Submit renewal requests to info@halalcert.com.ng — and with the new portal, you'll receive automatic reminders before expiry.

Apply online

Start your certification application

Complete the form and our team will respond with an evaluation and next steps. Prefer paper? You can still download the application form (PDF) and email it to info@halalcert.com.ng.

What you'll need

  • Company registration details and facility address.
  • Product list with full ingredient declarations.
  • Supplier halal certificates for animal-derived or sensitive ingredients.
  • Production process descriptions and sanitation SOPs.
