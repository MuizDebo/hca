Standards rooted in scholarship. Audits run by scientists.

HCA is a faith-based certification body developing halal product standards and conducting inspections in accordance with Islamic Law (Shariah) — internationally accepted and reliable for domestic and foreign consumers.

Who we are

An Islamic regulatory and quality assurance body

Halal Certification Authority develops halal product standards and accreditation procedures, and certifies products and services for both domestic consumption and export. Our work is grounded in Shariah and aligned with international halal standards.

  • Islamic regulatory body — standards rooted in Shariah scholarship.
  • Authorised certification & QA body — audits conducted by seasoned food scientists, Shariah experts and quality professionals.
  • Standards developer — halal product standards and accreditation processes for the Nigerian and regional market.

Our vision

To be the foremost halal certification body in Sub-Saharan Africa.

Our mission

To offer total halal certification services to companies and clients in accordance with Islamic Law (Shariah), using seasoned professionals and state-of-the-art technology for customer satisfaction while meeting stakeholders' expectations.

Objectives

  • Develop halal product standards in conformity with Islamic Law and international standards.
  • Offer halal certification services for products and services.
  • Develop human capacity in the halal industry.
  • Ensure traceability of certified products through technology.
  • Collaborate with public and private stakeholders to strengthen the halal economy.

The concept of halal

More than a label
