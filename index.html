What we do

Standards, inspection, and education for the halal economy

Halal certification

End-to-end certification of products, premises and processes — from application review to on-site audit and certification decision.

Education & training

Halal awareness, internal auditor and compliance training for QA teams, food handlers and certification professionals.

Consultancy

Guidance on halal-compliant sourcing, formulation, production line separation, and export-market requirements.

Promotion

Promoting certified halal products to consumers, retailers and export partners across Sub-Saharan Africa and beyond.

The certification process

Five steps from application to certificate

A defined, auditable sequence — no shortcuts, no self-declaration, no AI-generated approvals.

Application

Submit your company, facility and product details online or by form.

Evaluation

HCA reviews your application, ingredients and documentation.

Agreement & quote

Certification agreement signed and price quote issued.

On-site audit

Document review, step-by-step plant inspection, and audit report.

Decision

The Halal Certification Committee grants or declines certification.
